Your treatment journey

1. Consultation

We assess your skin, discuss your goals and confirm the right treatment and number of sessions.

2. Treatment

Relax while your treatment is performed with care, and with comfort measures where needed.

3. Aftercare

You'll go home with clear aftercare instructions and product recommendations to protect your results.

Before your appointment

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for 2 weeks.
  • Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids 5–7 days before.
  • Shave the area 24 hours before laser hair removal.
  • Let us know about any medication or skin conditions.
